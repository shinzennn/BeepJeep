# Project Specification: Beepjeep

## Overview
Beepjeep is a modern jeepney tracking and management application designed for three user roles: Drivers, Commuters, and Admins. The app handles live location tracking, passenger/fare management, and financial reporting.

## Visual & Design Guidelines
*   **Color Palette:** Mainly White and Orange (Clean, modern transit aesthetic).
*   **UI Style:** Simple, highly readable, and mobile-responsive for drivers and commuters on the go.

---

## Component & Page Requirements

### 1. Login Component
*   **Role Selection:** Clear option to log in or simulate access as an Admin, Driver, or Commuter.
*   **Authentication:** Basic form fields (Username/Password or simplified profile switching for prototyping).

### 2. Admin Page (Dashboard)
*   **Live Fleet Map:** A real-time, interactive map display showing the simulated or exact coordinates of all active jeepneys on the road.
*   **Financial & Boundary Reports:** A centralized dashboard aggregating daily fare collections, passenger breakdowns, and boundary metrics from all active drivers.

### 3. Driver Page
*   **Fare Tracker:** Interface allowing the driver to record passenger boardings based on type (e.g., Regular, Senior/Student discount).
*   **Jeepney Capacity State:** Simple toggle or counter indicating if the jeepney is "Full" or has "Seats Available."
*   **Earnings Report:** A personal summary showing daily income totals and passenger demand trends.

### 4. Commuter Page
*   **Live Jeepney Tracking:** Interactive map allowing the commuter to track live jeepney locations, view available routes, and check fixed fare costs.
*   **Proximity Alerts (Passenger Visibility):** Dual-notification system that alerts the commuter when a jeepney is approaching their location, and simultaneously alerts the approaching driver of a waiting passenger.
*   **Reporting & Feedback:** A simple rating and review system to report issues or rate the jeepney/driver experience.
